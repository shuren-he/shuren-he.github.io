Shuren He

Ph.D. Student in Statistics

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Statistics at Texas A&M University, advised by Huiyan Sang . My research interests include Bayesian machine learning, tree-based models, spatial statistics, causal inference, and Bayesian nonparametrics.

My research focuses on developing flexible statistical learning methods for structured and complex data.
